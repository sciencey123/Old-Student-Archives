Turning old notebooks into shared knowledge

Old Student Archives collects handwritten notes from students' previous years' rough notebooks, scans them into searchable PDFs, and then responsibly recycles the physical notebooks — so the knowledge lives on and the paper doesn't go to waste.

Why this exists

Every year, thousands of rough notebooks, full of diagrams, worked examples and last-minute exam notes, end up thrown away once a student moves on. That's lost learning material and wasted paper at the same time. We fix both problems at once: the notes get digitized and made searchable for future students, and the physical notebook is recycled instead of binned.
